How the module is structured

Simple stats stay flat. Magnetic, Reflect, RandomEffect and Invisible add named settings beside their value. Damage-over-time effects use Damage, Interval and Duration inside their own table. HealthDamageBoost uses HealthPercent, DamagePercent and Condition.

Percentages are exported directly: 10 = 10%, with no automatic division. DamageMultiplier is a multiplier instead: 1.2 = 20% more damage.

Your Roblox item system needs to read these exact keys and settings to apply the effects.
